# antisemitism_classifier
Designing an ML Classification Method to Identify Evolving and Complex Expressions of Antisemitism in Online Discourse

## Project Description

As noted by Salhi and Goldhorn (2025), antisemitism is a rapidly evolving discourse in the internet age. The usage of emoji, visual and textual memes, conspiratorial tropes, hashtags, irony, plausible deniability, and other complex semantic and semiotic constructions are being developed and circulated to spread and obscure antisemitic discourse. Building off of the work of Kennedy et al. (2020), this project seeks to specifically identify antisemitic text in a variety of contexts due to its uniquely volatile and mercurial nature, as well as identifying why a text is or is not antisemitic across varying definitions of antisemitism such as the IHRA and JDA definitions.
